Stepwise Kitchen Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Applies to the Stepwise Kitchen app for Android (com.basitmurad.stepwisekitchen)

In short: Stepwise Kitchen does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and does not connect to the internet.

Information we collect

None. Stepwise Kitchen does not collect any personal or device information, and it doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other data.

Information stored on your device

When you save a recipe, Stepwise Kitchen stores the recipe's ID in the app's private storage on your phone so your list is there next time. This information:

  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Internet access and third parties

Stepwise Kitchen does not request internet access. All recipes, images, and fonts are built into the app. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services, so no data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

Stepwise Kitchen does not request any Android permissions that give it access to personal data.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and to fix problems you report. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share your email with anyone. You can ask us to delete our emails with you at any time.

Children's privacy

Stepwise Kitchen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Stepwise Kitchen changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
