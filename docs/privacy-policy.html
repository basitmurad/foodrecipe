Stepwise Kitchen Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Applies to the Stepwise Kitchen app for Android (com.basitmurad.stepwisekitchen)

In short: Stepwise Kitchen has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your saved recipes stay on your phone. The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information the app itself stores

When you save a recipe, Stepwise Kitchen stores the recipe's ID in the app's private storage on your phone so your list is there next time. This information:

  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

We (the developer) do not run any servers for the app and do not receive any personal information from it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stepwise Kitchen shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash data.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About → Ad privacy settings.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all data collection by the app and its ads.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to load ads. All recipes, images and fonts are built into the app and work offline.

Permissions

Stepwise Kitchen requests internet access (to load ads) and access to the advertising ID (used by Google AdMob). It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and to fix problems you report. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share your email with anyone. You can ask us to delete our emails with you at any time.

Children's privacy

Stepwise Kitchen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Stepwise Kitchen changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
